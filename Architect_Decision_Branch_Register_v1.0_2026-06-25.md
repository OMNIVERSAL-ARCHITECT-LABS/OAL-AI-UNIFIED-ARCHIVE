## Purpose

This document converts the current development record into an exhaustive decision-question register. It is designed for iterative roleplay: I ask a question, Nathan answers, I ask two follow-up questions based on that answer, and then we project the short-, medium-, and long-term impact of that branch on the project.

The document intentionally distinguishes unresolved questions from items that may already have been answered but were not recorded accurately. It includes normal development questions, critical blocker questions, governance questions, narrative questions, system-integration questions, and wild-card questions that are difficult but useful because they expose hidden contradictions before they become expensive to repair.

## How to Use This Document

For each entry, run a three-question branch:

1. **Iteration 1 — Anchor:** Establish the core decision.
2. **Iteration 2 — Constraint:** Test boundaries, exceptions, costs, or failure cases.
3. **Iteration 3 — Consequence:** Decide how the answer changes other parts of the canon.

After the third answer, mark the item as one of the following: **Resolved**, **Partially Resolved**, **Deferred**, **Withheld**, or **Rejected / Non-Canon**.

## Decision Branch Template

- **Question ID:** Stable reference number.
- **Project Area:** Where the question belongs.
- **Status Audit:** Open, partial, stale, resolved-but-unrecorded, or wildcard.
- **Iteration 1:** The first decision question.
- **Branch A / B / C:** Plausible answer directions.
- **Iteration 2:** Follow-up question to narrow the answer.
- **Iteration 3:** Consequence question to lock downstream impact.
- **Impact Projection:** Short-term, medium-term, and long-term effects.
- **Resolution Output:** What should be written into the Master Change Log after the roleplay.
