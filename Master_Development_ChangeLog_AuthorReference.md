**Status:** Active — High-Concept Architectural Phase (Narrative Execution Pending)

| v0.1 | Pre-2026 | Core concept established |

### Author's Notes

**What's confirmed and solid:**
- Core artifact: psychologically encoded euphoric catalyst that accelerates cognition and civilization
- The Puritan Validation Event creates immediate dramatic tension
- Escalation ladder: Personal memory → corporate theft → civilizational case study → multi-species temporal arms race
- Universal compatibility (biological and digital systems)
- Quasi-sentient properties: the memory "hides," "shifts," "wants," "calls others"

**Critical gaps (Tier 1 — story cannot proceed without answers):**
1. Encoding mechanism: How does a subjective euphoric experience become transferable, cross-substrate data?
2. The Infiltrator: Who/what are they? Motivation? What happened in 17.9 seconds?
3. Temporal mechanics rules: Single vs. branching timelines? Paradox prevention? Costs?
4. Protagonist: Who is the primary POV character?
5. Cost / negative consequences: What goes wrong? What do accelerated civilizations lose?

**Scientific grounding available:**
- Engram neurons, ephaptic coupling, synaptic architecture → encoding mechanism
- DNA storage (215 petabytes/gram) → biological persistence and heritability
- Nanoparticle BBB traversal → universal compatibility mechanism
- Horizontal gene transfer / synthetic transposons → viral-like propagation
- Epigenetic inheritance → transgenerational transmission
- Kardashev Scale → mapping "Levels" of civilizational advancement

**Wattpad strategy:**
- Lead with character, not concept
- Mystery/thriller framing — open with the theft
- Epistolary elements (research logs, security transcripts, AI comms)
- Consider romantic subplot (flagged by all AIs)
- Target: 1,500–3,000 words/chapter, post 1–3x/week
- Marketing hook: *"What if your most intimate memory was stolen and used to start a war across time itself?"*

**Comparable fiction to study:**
- Snow Crash (Stephenson) — single vector across biological and digital substrates
- Neuromancer (Gibson) — abstract tech made visceral through character
- Illuminae (Kaufman/Kristoff) — epistolary format, YA appeal
- Altered Carbon (Morgan) — consciousness-as-data identity horror
- Flowers for Algernon (Keyes) — epistolary cognitive transformation

**Three immediate next steps:**
1. Answer the five Tier 1 questions (even rough paragraph answers)
2. Choose entry point scene (theft / Puritan board hearing / first AI exposure / integration experience / time-as-weapon realization)
3. Lock genre and tone — 2–3 primary genres
