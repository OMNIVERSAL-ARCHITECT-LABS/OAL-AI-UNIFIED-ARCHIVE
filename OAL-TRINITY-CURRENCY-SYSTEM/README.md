A version-controlled, multi-AI experimental research record for the OAL Trinity Currency System, examining Quantum Credits (QC), 1COIN, and NCOIN as provisional value-resolution, settlement, and countervalue architectures. This folder preserves source records, constitutional baselines, laboratory protocols, independent AI experiments, adversarial cross-reviews, boundary-condition testing, decision records, and evidence used to determine whether the proposed instruments should remain distinct, merge, be redesigned, or be rejected. The project is intentionally pre-canon: agreement among collaborators is not treated as validation, unresolved architectural differences are preserved as testable alternatives, and formal specification follows experimental evidence rather than preceding it.
