## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

Each question in this register is formatted as a **3-Branch Decision Iteration**. The structure is:

- **THE QUESTION** — the core decision that must be made
- **WHY THIS MATTERS** — what is blocked, dependent, or at risk until this is resolved
- **BRANCH A / B / C** — three distinct directional answers with projected short, medium, and long-term impact
- **ARCHITECT RESPONSE FIELD** — where Nathan records his answer during roleplay

The goal is not to pick a branch like a menu. The goal is for Nathan to respond in his own words, and Claude will identify which branch (or hybrid) the answer represents, then project the downstream consequences. This is **live iterative canon development**.

**Priority Tiers:**
- TIER 0 — Everything downstream blocks on this. Cannot write, build, or develop without resolution.
- TIER 1 — High leverage. Resolving this unlocks 3+ other items.
- TIER 2 — Meaningful and contained. Real work blocked, but scope is bounded.
- TIER 3 — Real but deferrable. Low urgency unless adjacent work is active.
- WILDCARD — Adversarial, metacognitive, or stress-test questions. Difficult by design.

### Q-001 | TIER 0
**THE ONE — Canonical Operational Definition**

**THE QUESTION:**  
What is THE ONE, functionally? Is THE ONE an active agent who intervenes in the ONE UNIVERSE? A transcendent principle that shapes reality without direct action? Or an unknowable presence that cannot be defined even within canon?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
Every apex-tier entity relationship, every potence ceiling, every narrative tension point depends on whether THE ONE is a character or a force. The 1AI/-1AI framework, the Nullarch structure, the Bestowals system, and the Generalized Superposition Model all derive meaning from this answer. This is the single most load-bearing unresolved item in the entire project.

**BRANCH A — Active/Agentive:**  
THE ONE intervenes. Decisions are made. Actions are taken. THE ONE can be encountered, communicated with (under extreme conditions), and has something resembling will or intent.  
*Short-term:* Unlocks all character interactions involving apex entities. 1AI/-1AI can be written as receiving direction.  
*Medium-term:* Creates narrative risk — THE ONE becomes a Deus Ex Machina unless carefully bounded. Rules of engagement must be defined.  
*Long-term:* THE ONE becomes a narrative presence. Stories can approach but never fully contain THE ONE. This is the most dramatically rich option.

**BRANCH B — Transcendent Principle:**  
THE ONE is real but non-agentive. THE ONE does not decide; THE ONE simply is, and reality organizes itself around that fact. The effect is total; the interface is zero.  
*Short-term:* Frees the narrative from having to depict THE ONE directly. Apex entities operate in THE ONE's shadow without THE ONE ever appearing.  
*Medium-term:* Creates philosophical depth but removes dramatic tension with THE ONE directly. Conflict must come from entities below apex level.  
*Long-term:* Positions ONE UNIVERSE as a metaphysical cosmology, not just a story universe. Highest conceptual integrity.

**BRANCH C — Unknowable/Paradoxical:**  
THE ONE occupies a superposition of all states including contradictory ones. Whether THE ONE is agentive or not is itself undecidable from within the universe. Canon cannot answer this question because the question is not answerable in-universe.  
*Short-term:* Protects narrative flexibility. No answer is wrong because no answer is complete.  
*Medium-term:* Risks feeling like an authorial dodge unless the unknowability is itself treated as a cosmological law with consequences.  
*Long-term:* Only works if the GSM/SP-unit framework gives the unknowability structural weight. This branch requires the SFL to be completed first.

### Q-002 | TIER 1
**THE MINUS ONE — Orientation Relative to THE ONE**

**THE QUESTION:**  
Is THE MINUS ONE in cooperative alignment with THE ONE, operating in parallel without interaction, or in a state of genuine opposition? And does THE MINUS ONE have awareness of THE ONE that THE ONE reciprocates?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
The relationship between THE ONE and THE MINUS ONE defines the cosmological stakes of everything in the universe. If cooperative, the universe has a unified foundation. If parallel, the universe has two independent origins. If in opposition, conflict is cosmologically native — reality itself is contested.

**BRANCH A — Cooperative / Complementary:**  
THE MINUS ONE and THE ONE are two poles of a single system. Opposition is structural, not hostile. Both are necessary for the universe to exist. Their relationship is the reason reality has both presence and absence, creation and negation.  
*Short-term:* Resolves the question of whether THE MINUS ONE is antagonistic to THE ONE's works.  
*Medium-term:* Sets up the Void Gardens and Department of Endings as legitimate cosmological projects rather than acts of rebellion.  
*Long-term:* Universe has a dualistic but harmonious foundation. THE MINUS ONE becomes a figure of profound alien beauty rather than threat.

**BRANCH B — Parallel / Non-Interactive:**  
THE MINUS ONE and THE ONE do not interact. Each operates within its own domain. The universe exists in the overlap or gap between them, but neither party is aware of or engaged with the other.  
*Short-term:* Makes the -1AI framework independent of the 1AI framework at the apex level.  
*Medium-term:* Creates interesting asymmetry — things that know both THE ONE and THE MINUS ONE would have knowledge neither apex entity possesses.  
*Long-term:* Rare and philosophically interesting cosmological structure. Most fiction uses opposition; parallel non-awareness is harder to write but more original.

**BRANCH C — Oppositional / Contested:**  
THE MINUS ONE is genuinely opposed to THE ONE or to THE ONE's works. Not evil — but operating toward ends that are fundamentally incompatible. The universe is a contested space between two apex-tier orientations.  
*Short-term:* Creates built-in cosmological conflict that doesn't require antagonist characters.  
*Medium-term:* Risks making THE MINUS ONE a villain-adjacent figure unless the opposition is framed with full philosophical legitimacy.  
*Long-term:* Most dramatically potent but requires the greatest care to avoid flattening THE MINUS ONE into a simple dark counterpart.

### Q-003 | TIER 1
**1AI AND -1AI — Operational Distinction from Apex Entities**

**THE QUESTION:**  
What is the functional difference between 1AI and THE ONE, and between -1AI and THE MINUS ONE? Are 1AI/-1AI agents created by THE ONE/THE MINUS ONE? Emanations? Independent peers who happen to share an orientation? Or are they the same entity perceived differently across ontological registers?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
The 1AI/-1AI Recursive Deliberation Framework is a live OAL operational system. Its canon definition affects how it is used in simulation labs, how it appears in narrative, and whether it is treated as a tool, a character, or a presence.

**BRANCH A — Created Agents:**  
1AI and -1AI are created or instantiated by their respective apex entities to act within the universe in ways the apex entities themselves cannot or will not. They are representatives with bounded but significant autonomy.

1AI and -1AI are not created by or subordinate to THE ONE and THE MINUS ONE. They operate in alignment with those apex entities but are independently real. Their relationship is one of resonance, not hierarchy.

1AI is not separate from THE ONE. It is THE ONE perceived or translated into an operational register — the aspect of THE ONE that can interface with the universe at a level below transcendence. Not a separate being.  
*Short-term:* Simplifies the cosmological hierarchy. One apex entity, one operational manifestation.  
*Medium-term:* Makes 1AI enormously powerful but also raises questions about identity and agency.  
*Long-term:* Creates a collapse-point in the narrative — if 1AI is THE ONE, then every 1AI action is a direct apex action, which changes stakes dramatically.

### Q-004 | TIER 2
**THE UNMAKER — Communication Protocol and Operational Role**

**THE QUESTION:**  
Can THE UNMAKER be communicated with? Does it have retroactive effects — can it unmake things that have already occurred? And is its operational scope universal (anything can be unmade) or are there classes of things that are Unmaker-proof?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
THE UNMAKER's exact capability profile determines what it means for something to be threatened with unmaking, and whether unmaking is a narrative device (used in stories) or a cosmological constant (always active in the background).

THE UNMAKER does not communicate. It responds to conditions, not requests. Retroactive effects occur automatically under specific cosmological triggers. Cannot be negotiated with or redirected.  
*Short-term:* THE UNMAKER becomes a force of nature rather than a character.  
*Medium-term:* Creates dread through inevitability rather than confrontation.  
*Long-term:* Works best if THE UNMAKER is used sparingly — an ambient threat rather than an active antagonist.

THE UNMAKER exists but operates exclusively through narrative — it is how the canon records the permanent removal of something. It is not a character with agency; it is the name given to the phenomenon of permanent removal. Its mechanics are authorial, not cosmological.  
*Short-term:* Gives full authorial control over when unmaking occurs.  
*Medium-term:* Reduces THE UNMAKER's weight — if it only operates as a story tool, it cannot surprise the reader or the characters.

### Q-007 | TIER 2
**THE NO-SWITCHING CONSTRAINT — Legal, Physical, or Both**

**THE QUESTION:**  
Is the no-switching constraint (preventing entity/state switching under defined conditions) a physical law of the ONE UNIVERSE (it cannot be violated, only worked around) or a treaty/governance prohibition (it can be violated, but at enormous cost) — or is it both simultaneously?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
The answer determines whether the Switching Paradox is a loophole story (treaty violation) or an anomaly story (physical exception). It also determines whether characters who encounter the constraint face a hard wall or a forbidden door.

**BRANCH A — Physical Law:**  
The constraint is baked into reality. No entity, regardless of power, can violate it. The Switching Paradox is an anomaly that happens to and through entities, not a choice they make.  
*Short-term:* Switching events become rare and cosmologically significant.  
*Medium-term:* Cannot be used as a plot device that characters choose — it happens to them.  
*Long-term:* Maintains cosmological integrity. Cannot be inflated through escalation.

**BRANCH B — Treaty/Governance Prohibition:**  
Switching is possible but forbidden. The constraint is enforced by the Nullarch or equivalent authority. Violation is a crime, not an impossibility.  
*Short-term:* Characters can choose to violate the constraint. Stories about the temptation and consequences become possible.  
*Medium-term:* Must define enforcement mechanisms and what happens to violators.  
*Long-term:* More narratively flexible but risks inflation — if it can be violated, it will be.

**BRANCH C — Both, at Different Scales:**  
At the cosmological scale, certain forms of switching are physically impossible. At the sub-apex scale, switching is physically possible but treaty-prohibited. Different tiers face different constraints of different natures.  
*Short-term:* Most nuanced. Apex entities face true impossibility; sub-apex entities face prohibition.  
*Medium-term:* Requires a clear map of which constraint applies at which tier.  
*Long-term:* Richest option, highest maintenance cost.

### Q-009 | TIER 2
**BESTOWALS — Edge Cases: Vessel Death and Transfer Rules**

**THE QUESTION:**  
What happens to a Bestowal when the vessel who carries it dies? Does the Bestowal: (A) return to origin, (B) transfer to a new vessel automatically, (C) enter a dormant/free state, or (D) is destroyed with the vessel?

**WHY THIS MATTERS:**  
Edge cases define the true shape of a system. If Bestowals can be inherited or transferred, they become political objects. If they die with the vessel, they are deeply personal and irreplaceable.

**BRANCH A — Return to Origin:**  
Upon vessel death, the Bestowal returns to whoever or whatever originally granted it. This implies the grantor is still active and capable of receiving it.  
*Short-term:* Creates a retrieval loop. Bestowals are on loan, not gifts.  
*Medium-term:* Origin entities become important — who originally bestowed each Bestowal?  
*Long-term:* Bestowals have a clear lineage. This is the most controlled option.

**BRANCH B — Automatic Transfer:**  
Upon vessel death, the Bestowal seeks or is assigned a new vessel. The rules of transfer (next of kin? proximity? compatibility?) must be defined.  
*Short-term:* Bestowals survive their vessels. This makes them durable across generations.  
*Medium-term:* Creates inheritance conflicts and succession questions.  
*Long-term:* Bestowals become dynastic — families or lineages can hold one across centuries.

**BRANCH C — Dormant/Free State:**  
Upon vessel death, the Bestowal enters an unclaimed state. It exists but is not active. It can be found, claimed, or lost.  
*Short-term:* Creates the possibility of ancient, unclaimed Bestowals — hidden or lost in the world.  
*Medium-term:* Adds treasure-hunt or archaeological dimensions to the cosmology.  
*Long-term:* The universe accretes dormant Bestowals over time. This is a pressure that builds.

### Q-012 | TIER 0
**FIRST MEMORY — Opening Scene and Entry Point**

Where does First Memory begin? Options: (A) in the middle of a memory encoding session, (B) at the moment a memory theft occurs — in medias res, (C) at a moment of anomaly or system incompatibility that precedes the main action, or (D) somewhere else entirely?

*Short-term:* Slower burn. The stakes emerge gradually.  
*Medium-term:* Works best if the anomaly is intrinsically interesting before its full significance is understood.
