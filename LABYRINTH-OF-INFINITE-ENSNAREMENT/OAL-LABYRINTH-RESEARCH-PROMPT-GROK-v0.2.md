### Domain B — Adaptive Breach-Learning Containment (working label only)
Research the Architect’s designed-to-fail scene under three hypotheses:  
1. Experimental containment: nested or sequential test environments designed to fail safely inside a stronger research boundary.  
2. Operational adaptive containment: genuine incidents provoke cautious, measured revisions.  
3. Deceptive or simulated escape: the entity appears to leave but remains within a controlled parent environment.  

For each iteration specify: baseline entity model; objective and hypothesis; environment design and boundaries; instrumentation and independent witness; anticipated breach paths; event log and causal reconstruction; stop/rollback criteria; and what counts as learning versus merely increasing restriction. Build a breach-and-learning matrix of at least 12 conceptual trials with distinct hypotheses and failure classes. Address observer effects, Goodhart’s law, specification gaming, and adaptive deception by the entity.
