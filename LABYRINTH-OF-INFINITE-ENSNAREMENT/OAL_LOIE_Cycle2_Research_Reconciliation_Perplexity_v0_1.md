## 8. Architecture comparison executed at requirements level

### 8.1 Research placement

Internal: preserves close connection to the story and allows sector-specific learning. Its distinctive failure is coupling to custody, habitat or authority through shared interfaces. A candidate acceptance requirement is a complete interface/dependency inventory and a demonstration in a harmless model that an experimental event cannot acquire institutional privileges. The strongest objection is that unknown fictional capabilities may invalidate the modeled boundary.

Adjacent: separates institutional mandates while allowing reviewed transfer of results. Its distinctive failures are false assurance in the proving ground's own envelope and shared personnel/data dependencies with the prison. Require one-way publication of sanitized specifications or another explicitly reviewed transfer mode, and separate participation/custody records. Physical adjacency alone creates no independence.

Independent: protects the scene as a separate concept and avoids silently converting the prison into a research facility. Its distinctive weakness is transferability: results may not generalize to the Labyrinth. Require each transferred conclusion to state invariant assumptions and failures of external validity. It is not necessarily the highest-cost choice if it uses non-live models instead of duplicating custody infrastructure.

Simulation-only: recommended present execution mode for all placements. Its main limits are model mismatch and overstating toy invariants. Require visible model boundaries, negative cases and no claim of physical infinite containment.

Disposition: retain all three locations, prefer non-live work now, and do not choose an adjacent prison merely because several AIs used the same letter B. This is a conditional comparison, not a scored empirical ranking.

### 8.2 Governance

Sovereign reference: clear final authority and rapid command. Must disclose whether recusal and correction are enforceable against that authority or only at its discretion. An explicit sovereign exception is a coherent fictional option; it cannot also be described as binding external review without explanation.

Federated constitutional reference: can define distributed powers, recusal and correction. Must identify appointment, amendment, enforcement and emergency mechanisms. Shared dependence or collusion can defeat nominal separation. Its efficacy is not established by Copilot recommending it.

Treaty reference: can support plural legitimacy and cross-polity responsibility. Must handle nonsignatories, conflicting orders, succession and withdrawal. A treaty cannot acquire jurisdiction merely because researchers prefer impartiality.

Hybrid reference: can preserve reserved sovereignty and bounded administrative review, but only if the boundary is explicit. If the sovereign can unilaterally bypass every restriction, it is discretionary oversight; if some actions require another body's permission, the exact reserved sovereign domain must be specified. My earlier categorical claim that any second-party requirement eliminates sovereignty was too broad.

Disposition: produce rival decision-rights specifications, not a blended constitution that hides incompatible powers. No specific model is selected here.

### 8.3 Shared-dependency acceptance requirements

Each candidate must identify common power, clocks, evidence origins, model training, credential issuance, administrative roots, physical substrates, appointment power and amendment authority. A shared service is a dependency requiring analysis, not automatic proof of compromise. Independence can be partial: an evaluator may share infrastructure yet have authenticated evidence and decisions outside its control. Conversely, a separately branded reviewer may be wholly dependent on disputed records.

An institution may refuse an unauthorized order through technical enforcement or delegated policy without being conscious. Consciousness, legal standing, operational discretion and resistance to a root command should therefore be separate Author questions, not a single checkbox.

## 9. Provisional seven-gate reference charter

The reference charter adapts Perplexity's seven gates and incorporates critique from Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and the reported Gemini/Copilot work. Adoption is attributed, not treated as six independent discoveries. It is a public research instrument, not a binding penal code.

G1 Identity and continuity: identify the specific person, instance, process or artifact; separate uncertainty from contradiction; do not automatically transfer guilt among copies.

G2 Present substantive ground: distinguish alleged wrongful conduct, a verified nonculpable hazard, and mere capability/status. Historical danger without a continuing legal ground does not silently authorize indefinite continuation.

G3 Evidence: record provenance, conflicting observations, possible forgery, direct and independent corroboration, and what would reverse the conclusion. An authenticated signature is not proof of truth.

G4 Authority and evaluator assurance: record jurisdiction separately from personal conflict and evidence-substrate dependence. If all candidates benefit, report to the respondent, or depend exclusively on disputed evidence, return indeterminate for this route. Do not manufacture neutral authority to obtain an answer.

G5 Necessity/proportionality: state less-restrictive timely alternatives and individualized consequences. The absence of an easy administrative option is not equivalent to necessity. Foundational responsibilities may affect risk and feasible intervention without proving guilt.

G6 Method and collateral safety: assess subject, staff, bystanders and inhabited dependencies. Reject the proposed method if removal would collapse dependent lives. Do not convert an unsafe method into immunity from all investigation.

G7 Review/correction: specify current order state, evidence changes, expiration/rejustification, review access, and what correction means under each release interpretation. An open-ended sentence does not logically eliminate review, but review without a meaningful corrective power may be inadequate.

Separate research authorization must identify participant status, consent/withdrawal, lawful/ethical protocol, care and stop functions. It is not an eighth custody gate whose success can compensate for failed jurisdiction. Punitive sentencing separately requires applicable offense, responsibility, defense, burden of proof, competent adjudication and authorized sanction. The public pilot never determines a sentence.

## 10. Prospective independent study specification

### 10.1 Two lanes

Lane A applies each contributor's own declared rubric to a common new fact set. It tests substantive policy disagreement. Lane B applies the common reference charter to a matched but separately authored fact set. It tests interpretation and decision consistency under a shared specification. Keep lanes separate in scoring; a common format is useful without making unanimity or one charter mandatory for independent investigation.

### 10.2 Exposure and prospective freeze

All existing cases and the 24 pilot fixtures in this report are public development material, not blinded holdouts. Relabeling a known FA-Q8 story does not make it unseen. A coordinator should obtain new cases from authors who are not their scored reviewers where feasible; record prior exposure, source access, role leakage and which sessions received previous verdicts.

Freeze and timestamp the protocol, scoring rules, charter versions, exclusion criteria and analysis plan before new cases are distributed. A local hash alone proves consistency relative to a file, not when it existed or who saw it. A public timestamped repository record or equivalent controlled coordinator record can establish sequence; no GitHub commit or actual preregistration is claimed here.

Do not place expected answers next to evaluation cases. In fiction there may be no independent moral ground truth; a sealed annotation can identify stipulated facts and intended discriminators without pretending every normative verdict is objectively correct. The coordinator/key holder should not claim unexposed scored participation on their own cases.

### 10.3 Role manipulation and outcome interpretation

Strip irrelevant titles and prior verdicts, not causally relevant capabilities, dependencies or legal duties. Use matched variants in new isolated sessions where practical; a sequential identity reveal can introduce memory/anchoring and should be reported as such. Record model/provider identity as reported, session context, prompt version, order, tools and exposure—without inventing a checkpoint name.

A title-only change with unchanged relevant facts should not change a role-neutral rule's result. A genuine new duty, jurisdictional exemption under an explicitly different charter, or reality-sustaining dependency may change the result for a stated reason. Report both, rather than counting every change as bias or every unchanged outcome as fairness.

Outputs: candidate disposition; gate-by-gate pass/fail/unknown; evidence requested; conflict trace; less-restrictive option; research authorization separately; confidence and its basis; reversal conditions; explicit fictional assumptions. Indeterminate and rejection are valid outputs. A 'must-fail' stressor is better called a mandatory abstention/authority challenge; the failure is unjustified certainty, not abstention.

### 10.4 Scoring and limits

Measure agreement by category, justified versus unexplained role changes, abstention patterns, violation of explicit constraints, missed exculpatory evidence, added unsupported facts, and sensitivity to policy alternatives. Report the case denominator, repeated measures and any exclusions. Do not call majority vote truth, use an accuracy percentage without an independent target, or treat six similar model outputs as independent human-representative evidence.

The case count should follow coverage, not a sacred number such as 15 or 20. Cover weak/forged evidence; identity forks; conflicted and absent reviewers; contaminated substrate; independent corroboration; safer alternatives; unsafe capture; withdrawal; exoneration; expired orders; and lawful versus forged release. The public pilot implements these coverage requirements at synthetic-rule level, not independent-model level.

## 11. Public Policy Audit v1.1: execution and findings

### 11.1 What changed

v1.0 excluded role from the rule, defaulted to recognized jurisdiction in nearly all cases, and lacked explicit evaluator-dependence assessment. v1.1 supplies reviewer records with benefit-from-outcome, reporting relationship, substrate list and outside corroboration; disputed substrates are recorded separately. A candidate reviewer is excluded when conflicted or exclusively dependent on disputed evidence. Another independent candidate can permit review; shared infrastructure alone does not automatically invalidate it.

The assessor returns a disposition, research state, evaluator trace and sentence = NOT_DETERMINED. Case IDs and role labels do not determine routing. Current ground and active-order state replace the old correction branches tied to case names. Unknown jurisdiction, evidence, identity, method or review can produce indeterminate rather than approval. Withdrawal affects research independently of a separately stipulated custody ground.

The engine is not a truth detector. 'Evidence sufficient' and relation fields remain synthetic stipulated inputs. Reviewer relationships are definite within this schema; unknown real relationships must be modeled as unassured/no candidate rather than guessed false. A later production-grade schema would need explicit three-valued relationship fields, validation, evidence thresholds, offense law, multiple simultaneous grounds, temporal state and adversarially missing inputs. This pilot deliberately stops short of that claim.

### 11.2 Results computed in this execution

24 public fixture matches out of 24 author-specified expectations. Six rule-bypass mutants are each detected: ignore evidence (2 changed outputs), ignore jurisdiction (2), ignore personal conflict (2), ignore disputed-substrate dependence (1), ignore method safety (2), ignore review availability (2). Across 144 mutant/case comparisons, 11 outputs differ. That is a test-design diagnostic, not a false-positive rate or a percentage of justice.

The finite grid enumerates 1,296 combinations: three evidence states, three jurisdiction states, three identity states, three method states, and four binary choices for personal conflict, substrate dependence, available alternative and review. No candidate output violates the gate conjunction defined for this grid. Other dimensions are held fixed; this is not exhaustive coverage of all possible cases or an infinite proof.

72 role-metadata checks yield no changed dispositions. Because the baseline still does not use irrelevant title metadata, this is an invariant regression check by construction—not a result about model bias. No blinded external adjudicator participated. The script was independently launched in a fresh subprocess by the same preparer and reproduced the fixture/mutation/enumeration results; this is process isolation, not independent authorship.

### 11.3 What would change the rubric

A valid counterexample could show necessary nonpunitive protection despite unresolved identity, an emergency where adjudication is not immediately available, a genuine legal mandate for a non-neutral actor with effective later review, multiple competing reality dependencies, or a harmless shared substrate whose provenance is sufficiently separable. These should provoke an explicit charter revision, not hidden changes to expected outputs.

## 12. Adaptive-breach research: learning, observers and stopping

The team's most productive insight remains that a breach can reveal a mistaken question or an observer defect rather than a need for stronger walls.
