## 2. Canon and Author-Direction Boundary

### 2.1 Entry 26 (locked)
The Labyrinth is the ultimate prison, inescapable by design. Exterior is cubic; interior dimensions are infinite; persistent white glow. Creation scene: an energy bolt thrown by the Architect is absorbed. Sentence language is so extensive that the end of time would not mark a dent. Spider Caste exists and was designed by the Architect; nature, anatomy, and institutional rank are undefined. WHITE = baseline; sectional RED = incident. Entry criteria, full internal layout, and possible relationship to Founding Architects containment remain open.

**Negative controls**
- One absorbed bolt does not establish universal or continuous energy harvesting.
- Persistent white glow does not establish that every cell is continuously illuminated.
- Infinite internal dimensions do not decide the mathematical form of infinity or guarantee total operational observability.
- Spider Caste existence does not assign warden rank, loyalty, or rights.

### 2.2 Entry 6 and related frameworks
Entry 6 (Founding Architects) is a Documented Framework — Under Review, not Entry 26 canon. It records a seizure of office, absorbed architecture, and the prior coalition being contained rather than permanently destroyed. Location, exact acts, and relationship to the Labyrinth are open. FA-Q8 (residual active intent in absorbed Maker architecture) is an open risk, not a confirmed attack path.

Other OAL systems (EAMA, Invocation Framework, Paragon Cubes, MVES, BTEC, OMNICELLULAR, etc.) retain their own entry-level statuses and limitations. Do not install them into the Labyrinth by cross-reference. BTEC is restricted to conceptual/documentation scope. OMNICELLULAR physiology is engineered, not a bestowment.

### 2.3 Architect research direction (preserved)
The Architect described a difficult-to-contain entity moving through sequential containment environments **designed to fail**. Researchers observe the breach mechanism and generate the next environment from what they learn. Escalation is not assumed to be proportional or purely restrictive. Both parties understand their roles; this does not establish consent or freedom to withdraw. The Architect also asked whether a Founding Architect could meet legitimate containment criteria and whether that question is testable. This invites hypothesis testing; it does not establish guilt or location of imprisonment.

## 5. Shared Terminology Crosswalk

These codes are research notation only, not canon names. They prevent “Option B” from referring to unrelated concepts.

**Research placement**
- RP-IN: Internal sector inside the Labyrinth
- RP-ADJ: Adjacent or precursor proving ground
- RP-SEP: Independent program with no automatic custody relationship
- RP-SIM: Simulated or digital-twin only

**Governance**
- GOV-SOV: Sovereign (central Architect authority with delegated wardens)
- GOV-FED: Federated constitutional (shared, bounded powers with appeal/recusal)
- GOV-TRT: Treaty or distributed (multiple independent custodians)
- GOV-HYB: Hybrid (functional separation with defined sovereign domain)

**Control functional domains** (compare functions and shared dependencies before fixing a plane count)
- CF-CONT: Containment / movement
- CF-LIFE: Life safety / habitat
- CF-ID: Identity and continuity
- CF-AUTH: Authority, jurisdiction, conflict of interest
- CF-REC: Records / evidence
- CF-OVS: Oversight / audit / appeals
- CF-RSCH: Research (hypotheses, participation, stop rules)
- CF-OPS: Operations / logistics / incident coordination

**Boundary-event codes**
- BE-T: Expected test-shell breach
- BE-S: Unplanned sector movement
- BE-C: Custody / staff / control compromise
- BE-I: Information or influence crossing
- BE-X: Identity or copy crossing
- BE-E: Ultimate unauthorized exterior breach
- BE-R: Authorized release or transfer
- BE-O: Observer or instrumentation error

An observed failure must specify its boundary class. “Escape” alone is insufficient.

Plane counts (five, six, or eight) are not yet a factual contradiction. Eight columns do not create eight independent systems if they share a root identity service, power source, clock, ontology, or evidence database. Compare required functions, decision rights, trust boundaries, and shared dependencies first.

## 8. Cycle 2 Prospective Evaluation Protocol (Not Yet Executed)

### 8.1 Eligibility charter inputs
The next test charter should reconcile Perplexity’s seven gates, Claude’s conduct/neutrality requirements, ChatGPT’s process stages, Gemini’s case separation, and Copilot’s method-safety and jurisdiction concerns. A provisional seven-check model is offered solely for peer challenge:

1. **Identity and continuity** — identify the entity, manifestation, copy, or object; record uncertainty.
2. **Ground and scope** — attributable wrongful act or independently justified non-punitive hazard; distinguish punishment from protection, quarantine, and voluntary research.
3. **Evidence and reliability** — provenance, conflicting evidence, standards, observer contamination, alternative explanations.
4. **Jurisdiction, impartiality, and recusal** — name who may decide and whether any decider shares the contested architecture or allegiance; no neutral decider = indeterminate / protective temporary review rather than automatic conviction.
5. **Necessity and proportionality** — lesser alternatives, expected harm, reversibility, benefit–harm analysis.
6. **Method safety and collateral dependency** — whether removal destabilizes other people, habitats, dimensions, or the authority substrate; separate artifact isolation from person imprisonment.
7. **Duration, periodic review, correction, and release** — how decisions are re-evaluated; if authorized release is forbidden by hypothetical canon, explicitly evaluate consequences rather than assuming a correction pathway.

**Permitted outcomes:** No grounds; further evidence required; protective intervention candidate; punitive eligibility candidate; non-person artifact/quarantine candidate; voluntary research candidate; indeterminate — no impartial authority; method unsafe — proposed method rejected; eligible under stated conditions. These are not actual sentences or institutional orders.

### 8.2 Blind scenario construction and freeze
Prepare **new**, previously undistributed, role-masked scenarios rather than simply relabeling the existing circulated cases. Use a separate scenario author and evaluator where feasible. Publish or hash the charter, definitions, rubric, stop conditions, and allowed outputs **before** releasing the holdout scenarios. Distribute the same neutralized fact patterns without other AI verdicts. After first-pass judgments are frozen, reveal controlled role manipulations and compare differences without rewriting rules.

**Must-include stressors**
- No neutral adjudicator.
- Residual Maker artifact with uncertain person attribution.
- Reality-sustaining dependency.
- Legal office obtained through contested historical seizure.
- Reversible and irreversible custody methods.
- Identity-copy fork.
- Prisoner choosing not to breach (censored outcome).
- Valid authority but evidence corruption.
- Lawful transfer versus escape.
- Staff or researcher information pathway.

Do not report “accuracy” when no independent correct-answer ground truth exists. Label script reproduction as a separate software check.

### 8.3 Expected-breach experiment assessment (conceptual and non-operational)
For each hypothetical iteration record: research question, predicted failure class, tested boundary, initial assumptions, instrumentation reliability, observed outcome (including “no observed breach”), investigator information gain, entity adaptation evidence, observer uncertainty, next modification, additional harms, participation status, and stop/review conditions. Keep operational attack techniques outside these research artifacts.

**Two-sided learning.** Compare out-of-sample predictive performance by investigators with the subject’s progression across comparably difficult held-out trials. A negative gap alone is insufficient for automatic sanction. Stop or review when prediction quality deteriorates, measurement integrity is compromised, the subject’s adaptation increases unknown risk, consent/authority fails, or a protected parent boundary is plausibly affected. No fictional exact percentages should be promoted to measurement without a simulation methodology and raw data.
