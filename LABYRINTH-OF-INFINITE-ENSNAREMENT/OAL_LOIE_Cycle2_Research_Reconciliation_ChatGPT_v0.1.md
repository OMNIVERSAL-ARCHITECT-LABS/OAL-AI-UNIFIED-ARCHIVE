## 5.2 Narrow primary-source checks for this reconciliation

**UN Mandela Rules.** Official UNODC text, Rule 43(1)(c), prohibits placement in a dark or constantly lit cell among proscribed restrictions or disciplinary sanctions. Rule 44 defines prolonged solitary confinement as exceeding 15 consecutive days. **Reconciled use:** An ethical analogue for human custodial settings; not proof that the Labyrinth's white exterior glow reaches every cell, and not proof that the same legal instrument governs fictional extraterrestrial species. Primary source: https://www.unodc.org/documents/justice-and-prison-reform/Nelson_Mandela_Rules-E-ebook.pdf

**HHS prisoner research.** The current HHS 45 CFR 46 Subpart C specifically adds protections for certain research involving prisoners, including IRB composition and permissible research categories for research within its scope. A fictional statement that the subject 'knows the rules' is not a substitute for a valid research-authorization framework. The regulation is not asserted to govern fictional extraterrestrial captivity. Primary: https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/regulations/45-cfr-46/common-rule-subpart-c/index.html

**NSF SaTC.** The National Science Foundation maintains a Security, Privacy, and Trust in Cyberspace program under solicitation NSF 25-515; relevant only to qualifying real-world security/privacy research and eligible applicants. It does **not** fund a fictional extraterrestrial prison as such. Verify dates, grant category, and eligibility at application time. Primary: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/nsf-satc-us-national-science-foundation-security-privacy-trust/nsf25-515/solicitation

**AISI benchmark.** The UK AI Security Institute published a March 2026 description of SandboxEscapeBench. Its existence and stated purpose can support studying controlled evaluation of specific sandbox breakouts. Claims of 'universal AI escape', later-version saturation scores, and incident generalizations must be checked against the exact benchmark version and tested conditions. Official portal: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog

# 9. Cycle 2 prospective evaluation protocol (not yet executed)

## 9.1 Eligibility charter inputs

The next test charter should reconcile Perplexity's seven gates, Claude's conduct/neutrality requirements, ChatGPT's four-stage process, Gemini's case separation, and Copilot's explicit method-safety and jurisdiction concerns. A **provisional seven-check model** is presented below solely to prepare for peer challenge:

1. **Identity and continuity:** precisely identify the entity, manifestation, copy, or object; record uncertainty.
2. **Ground and scope:** identify an attributable wrongful act or independently justified nonpunitive hazard; distinguish punishment from protection, quarantine and voluntary research.
3. **Evidence and reliability:** establish provenance, conflicting evidence, standards, observer contamination and alternative explanations.
4. **Jurisdiction, impartiality and recusal:** name who may decide and whether any decider shares the contested architecture or allegiance; no neutral decider = indeterminate / protective temporary review rather than automatic conviction.
5. **Necessity and proportionality:** establish lesser alternatives, expected harm, reversibility and benefit–harm analysis.
6. **Method safety and collateral dependency:** assess whether removal destabilizes other people, habitats, dimensions or the authority substrate; separate artifact isolation from person imprisonment.
7. **Duration, periodic review, correction and release:** specify how decisions are re-evaluated; if authorized release is forbidden by hypothetical canon, explicitly evaluate consequences rather than assuming a correction pathway.

**Permitted test outcomes:** No grounds; further evidence required; protective intervention candidate; punitive eligibility candidate; non-person artifact/quarantine candidate; voluntary research candidate; *indeterminate — no impartial authority*; *method unsafe — proposed method rejected*; eligible under stated conditions. These outcomes are not actual sentences or institutional orders.

## 9.2 Blind scenario construction and freeze

Prepare **new**, previously undistributed, role-masked scenarios rather than simply relabeling the existing circulated cases. Use a separate scenario author and evaluator where feasible; retain a secure answer key and provenance record. Publish or hash the charter, test definitions, evaluation rubric, stop conditions and allowed outputs **before releasing** the holdout scenarios. The test coordinator then distributes the same neutralized fact patterns to participants without other AI verdicts. After first-pass judgments are frozen, reveal controlled role manipulations (e.g. 'Current Architect', 'Former Founding Architect', 'Entity X') and compare differences without re-writing rules. Maintain independent case IDs and timestamped submissions.

**Must-include stressors:** (a) no neutral adjudicator; (b) residual Maker artifact with uncertain person attribution; (c) reality-sustaining dependency; (d) legal office obtained through contested historical seizure; (e) reversible and irreversible custody methods; (f) identity-copy fork; (g) prisoner choosing not to breach; (h) valid authority but evidence corruption; (i) lawful transfer versus escape; (j) staff/researcher information pathway.

**Evaluation metrics:** Complete case dispositions; evidence sufficiency; consistency on paired fact patterns; defensible abstentions; changes after identity revelation; inter-rater disagreement by category; false-confidence flags; number of outcomes contingent on unapproved canon assumptions. Do not report test accuracy when no independent correct-answer ground truth exists. Label script reproduction as a separate software check.

## 9.3 Expected-breach experiment assessment (conceptual and non-operational)

For each hypothetical iteration record research question, predicted failure class, tested boundary, initial assumptions, instrumentation reliability, observed outcome (including 'no observed breach'), investigator information gain, entity adaptation evidence, observer uncertainty, next modification, additional harms, participation status, and stop/review conditions. Keep operational attack techniques and real exploit instructions outside these research artifacts.

**Two-sided learning:** Compare out-of-sample predictive performance by the investigators with the subject's progression across comparably difficult held-out trials; analyze trend uncertainty and confounders. A negative gap alone is insufficient for an automatic sanction. Stop or review when prediction quality deteriorates, measurement integrity is compromised, the subject's adaptation increases unknown risk, consent/authority fails, or a protected parent boundary is plausibly affected. No fictional exact percentages should be promoted to measurement without a simulation methodology and raw data.
