# Writing Prompts: The Unified Duality Framework
## Technical Documentation - Section by Section

---

## FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

**What you're documenting:** The Unified Duality Framework - a methodology for building and managing complementary AI systems that must operate as integrated pairs rather than independent entities.

**Core principle:** Certain AI systems (or paired systems) achieve optimal performance only when treated as unified wholes rather than separable components, even when those components appear to be in tension or opposition.

**Target audience:** AI developers, system architects, creative studios using AI for narrative consistency, research teams building multi-agent systems.

---

## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM (2-3 Pages)

### Your Writing Prompts

**Prompt 1: Identify the Core Problem**

Write 2-3 paragraphs answering:
- What goes wrong when people try to build complementary AI systems as separate, independent entities?
- What do they expect will happen vs. what actually happens?
- Why is this problem not obvious at first?

*Example starter:* "When building AI systems with complementary functions—such as a creative generation model paired with a critical evaluation model—the natural assumption is that each component should operate independently..."

---

**Prompt 2: Make It Concrete**

Create one detailed scenario (1 page) showing this problem in action:
- Give the scenario a context (company, project, specific goal)
- Show the decision to separate the systems
- Describe what went wrong
- Make it specific enough that readers recognize similar situations

*Template:*
"A [type of organization] was developing [specific AI application]. They built two models: one focused on [function A] and another on [function B]. The team assumed that running these separately would provide [expected benefit]. Instead, they encountered [specific problems]..."

---

**Prompt 3: Explain Why Traditional Solutions Fail**

Write 1-2 paragraphs addressing:
- What do people typically try when they encounter this problem?
- Why don't those solutions work?
- What's the deeper issue they're missing?

*Example:* "Most teams respond by adding synchronization layers, shared databases, or communication protocols between the systems. While these approaches reduce some friction, they don't address the fundamental issue: the systems were never meant to be separate in the first place..."

---

**Prompt 4: State the Stakes**

One paragraph on:
- What's the cost of getting this wrong?
- What opportunities are missed?
- Why should someone care about solving this?

## WRITING TIPS FOR SUCCESS

**1. Write Shitty First Drafts**
Don't edit while writing. Get words down. Polish later.

**2. Use Examples Constantly**
Every abstract principle gets a concrete example.

**3. Define Before Using**
First mention of any term includes definition.

**4. Show, Don't Just Tell**
"The system degraded" → "Response coherence dropped from 94% to 67% within 48 hours"

**5. Write for Someone Smart But Unfamiliar**
Assume intelligence, not prior knowledge.

**6. Take Breaks Between Sections**
Let each section rest before editing.

**7. Read Out Loud**
If you stumble reading it, readers will stumble too.

**8. Ask "So What?"**
After each major point, answer why it matters.
