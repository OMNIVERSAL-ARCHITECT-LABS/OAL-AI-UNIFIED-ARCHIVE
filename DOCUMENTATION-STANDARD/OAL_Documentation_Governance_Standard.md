Prepared By: Microsoft 365 Copilot (GPT-5)

## Executive Summary
This document reviews the submitted responses from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, and Copilot. The consensus is that the requirement is not a style guide but a documentation governance system. The independent execution adopts purpose-first documentation, mobile-first readability, mandatory PDF/DOCX/Markdown publication, exhibit governance, classification standards, AI attribution, and format-failure protocols.

## Independent Findings
1. Governance is more important than formatting.
2. Mobile readability must be a primary requirement.
3. Narrative must precede tables.
4. Documents require measurable purpose.
5. AI attribution must be mandatory.

## Independent Standard
### Purpose First Rule
Every document must identify purpose, audience, expected outcome, success criteria, and next action.

### Required Formats
Deliver PDF, DOCX, and Markdown. Content fidelity must remain consistent across all formats.

### Format Failure Protocol
If a format cannot be produced:
- State the missing format.
- State the reason.
- Deliver available formats.
- Provide conversion instructions.
- Mark publication status as FORMAT INCOMPLETE.

### Mandatory Metadata
Title, Version, Status, Organization, Classification, Preparer, Date, Timestamp, Audience, Purpose.

### Organizational Labels
OAL, One Industries, Lewis Corp, Joint Initiative, or External Reference.

### Reality Boundary Labels
Real-World, Conceptual, Simulated, Hypothetical, Fictional, Mixed.

### Mobile First Standard
Single-column reading experience. Avoid wide tables. Use short paragraphs and clear headings.

### Exhibit Governance
All tables, figures, charts, and diagrams shall be numbered and referenced by item number. Large exhibits belong in dedicated exhibit sections.

### AI Attribution Standard
AI systems must identify themselves and their role in document preparation.

### Publication Acceptance Gate
A document cannot be approved unless purpose, metadata, classification, exhibits, readability review, and required formats are complete.

## Conclusion
The strongest elements of all submissions converge on a single principle: documentation exists to support understanding, decisions, execution, and accountability. The recommended standard should govern behavior, publication quality, traceability, and readability rather than visual appearance alone.
