## Overview

This document compiles an exhaustive working register of unresolved, partially resolved, structurally missing, and architect-sensitive questions across the current Master Development and Change Log and related project materials.[cite:1][cite:2][cite:3][cite:4] It is designed as a reusable decision workbook rather than a static list, because several entries are already partially defined but still require explicit ratification, operational clarification, or scope control before downstream drafting can proceed cleanly.[cite:1][cite:2][cite:3]

The register distinguishes between three types of issues: direct open questions already named in the project record, structural gaps inferred from “needs development” or “pending” flags, and wild-card questions that are not explicitly logged but are likely to surface during long-horizon development, canon stabilization, publication, or adaptation.[cite:1][cite:2][cite:3][cite:4] The goal is not only to ask what remains unresolved, but to show how each answer can branch across short-, mid-, and long-term project impact.[cite:2][cite:3]

## Use Model

Each question entry follows a four-part structure.

- **Question:** The unresolved or pressure-test item.
- **Why it matters:** The reason the question affects canon, systems design, execution, publication, or narrative stability.
- **Three-iteration roleplay branch:** A simulation of how the decision conversation could develop over three rounds.
- **Impact horizon:** The likely short-, medium-, and long-term consequence of the answer path.

The three-iteration model is intentionally consistent across entries so it can be reused in live sessions with the Architect.[cite:2][cite:3] Iteration 1 forces a first-position answer, Iteration 2 introduces a pressure test or edge case, and Iteration 3 tests whether the answer survives integration with the broader project record.[cite:2][cite:3][cite:4]

## Priority tiers

The current record supports a practical tiering model.

| Tier | Meaning | Typical effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Hard blocker | Story, systems, or canon cannot move cleanly until answered.[cite:2][cite:3] |
| Tier 2 | Structural stabilizer | Work can continue, but contradiction risk or drift remains high.[cite:1][cite:2][cite:4] |
| Tier 3 | Expansion shaper | Affects depth, adaptation, presentation, or future scaling more than immediate movement.[cite:2][cite:3] |
| Wild Card | Difficult or adversarial question | Usually appears late, often under pressure, and can expose hidden contradictions.[cite:3][cite:4] |
