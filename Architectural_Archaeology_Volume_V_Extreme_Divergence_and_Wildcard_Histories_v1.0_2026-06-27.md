## A. The Conservator Unmakers
**Branch type:** Role inversion / ethical reversal

**Extreme premise:** Unmakers are conservation specialists for dangerous, unstable, or unfinished realities rather than permanent destroyers.

**Alternative present:** The archive gains a Conservation Clause and reframes Void Gardens, the Unmaker interface, and the Labyrinth around stewardship.

**Cascade:** Immediate: ending means controlled reduction, archive, reset, or protection.

Medium: accelerated civilizations become conservation risks.

Long: expansion versus stewardship replaces creation versus destruction.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: non-binary ethics of ending and preservation.

Risk: coercive containment can be renamed “care.”

Boundary: does not define the Unmaker or the contained Architects.

## B. The Residual Inheritance Mesh
**Branch type:** Distributed residual architecture

**Extreme premise:** Absorption never becomes perfectly clean; systems retain traceable residues such as design habits, constraints, and dormant preference structures.

**Alternative present:** The archive contains lineage anatomy alongside operational descriptions. Conditional Failure becomes inheritance drift; Paragon Cubes become federations of inherited logics.

**Cascade:** Immediate: a ratified succession event requires provenance audit.

Medium: research develops protocols for interacting with residual structures.

Long: the culture values responsible translation over purity.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: strong provenance discipline.

Risk: every inconsistency gets explained as hidden will.

Boundary: no actual residual intelligence is claimed.

## C. The Witness Memory
**Branch type:** Agency reassignment

**Extreme premise:** The First Memory functions as a witness system, not merely a catalyst.

**Alternative present:** The archive gains Memory Witness Records; theft becomes a break in testimony; Time Wars become disputes over editing civilization-scale remembered experience.

**Cascade:** Immediate: experiments record recognition and desire effects.

Medium: Accords develop memory-rights language.

Long: the narrative asks what civilizations owe experiences used to transform themselves.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: accountability and consent become central.

Risk: premature personification of an artifact.

Boundary: Tier-1 First Memory questions remain open.

## D. The Meaning Commons
**Branch type:** Post-scarcity value inversion

**Extreme premise:** Material capacity is abundant while attention, consent, continuity, belonging, and purpose are scarce.

**Alternative present:** Currencies become accountability instruments; living media becomes civic restoration infrastructure; the Department of Endings becomes a cultural institution.

**Cascade:** Immediate: compute cannot manufacture legitimate consent.

Medium: end-of-system processes focus on closure without erasure.

Long: abundance removes easy excuses for neglect.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: durable stakes beyond material scarcity.

Risk: “meaning” becomes coercive currency.

Boundary: actual currency governance remains open.

## E. The Distributed Labyrinth
**Branch type:** Networked containment

**Extreme premise:** Containment is a distributed grammar across realities, agreements, places, and systems rather than a single prison location.

**Alternative present:** The Labyrinth’s cube becomes an anchor for a universe-wide pathway-denial protocol; Spider Caste functions become monitoring and barrier maintenance.

**Cascade:** Immediate: escape becomes a question of closed pathways.

Medium: due process and restoration become essential.

Long: safety becomes non-lethal but potentially invasive.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: reversible harm prevention.

Risk: total surveillance and exclusion.

Boundary: does not identify the actual Labyrinth, Unmaker, or containment environment.

## F. The Untranslated Future
**Branch type:** Translation constraint

**Extreme premise:** The final untranslated material is a legitimate limit, not damaged data waiting to be solved.

**Alternative present:** The archive contains Untranslatability Notices and teaches differences among unread, withheld, unknown, and unformable.

**Cascade:** Immediate: collaborators must label interpretive limits.

Medium: Living Media becomes a translation laboratory.

Long: restraint becomes a capability.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: protects uncertainty and provenance.

Risk: mystery becomes avoidance.

Boundary: does not translate the rhyme or identify its speakers.

## G. The Reciprocal Apex
**Branch type:** Reciprocal structure

**Extreme premise:** Apex principles are structurally complete only through paired responsibilities: creation requires ending questions; negation requires preservation questions.

**Alternative present:** The archive adopts a Reciprocity Ledger. Power remains undiminished but consequences expand as responsibility and interpretation.

**Cascade:** Immediate: Park Scene rules become a category-limit lesson.

Medium: creators plan endings; unmakers preserve legibility.

Long: conflict centers on failures of reciprocal responsibility.

**Stress-test value:** Benefit: meaningful high-tier stakes without arbitrary weakening.

Risk: myth becomes over-mechanized.

Boundary: does not define final ontology or hierarchy.
