## 3. Project 1 — Ratified Decisions & Active Development Authority

### 3.1 Canon Scene Parameters — Locked

**The Kinetic Threshold Demonstration** takes place 3 years before the theft. It is a joint corp-gov classified event attended by military acquisition officers, the ONE Industries board, and a single ethics observer. The demonstration uses pre-scripted synthetic humanoids under Nathan's manual override authority. Beta's cycle initiates 0.4 seconds late during the third shot — Nathan's expression does not change. The third shot results in Beta being dramatically flung but clearly functional, demonstrating the armor's energy management limits under real variance.

One synthetic unit is retained by the government under contractual rights following the demonstration. Nathan does not object publicly.

**The Open Ocean Parley** occurs post-theft, reached via submersible. Nathan's colleague is a Black-Ops / Military Liaison with a defined motive, cost, leverage point, a reason they risk the meeting, and a reason they do not simply report Nathan. What changes hands: proof of government surveillance of ONE Industries. Nathan's outcome: a relational cost — loss of certainty about someone he trusted. A communication arrives during his absence that changes his next move upon return.

### 3.2 Canon Chain — Project 1 into Project 2

With Q10 confirmed as post-theft and DEP-1 confirmed, the canonical sequence is:

1. **The Kinetic Threshold Demonstration** (3 years pre-theft) — Nathan at peak public-facing power
2. **The theft** — the event that restructures Nathan's relationship with institutions
3. **The Open Ocean Parley** (post-theft, exact interval TBD) — Nathan receives proof of surveillance via submersible; loses certainty about a trusted relationship
4. **The Network Defense Architecture** is built in the post-theft period, motivated by the surveillance knowledge and the relational fracture from the Parley — this is DEP-1 resolved

The government already holds one of Nathan's synthetics (DEP-2) when it becomes Nathan's joint-task-force partner in Project 2. This creates a layered dynamic: Nathan works with a government that both surveils him and holds property derived from his work.

### 3.3 Confirmation Bias Flags — Preserved for Development Reference

- **Flag 1 (Omnipotence):** The 0.4-second anomaly beat (Q21) is the designed response. Nathan handles it with invisible control. ✦ Ratified.
- **Flag 2 (Colleague as delivery vehicle):** The colleague has their own agenda, cost, and leverage. The meeting has a cost to Nathan, not just a benefit. ✦ Ratified.
- **Flag 3 (Pre-theft default):** Retired. Post-theft is now canon. ✦ Ratified.
- **Flag 4 (Synthetic ethics as background):** The retained synthetic (Q20) is the mechanism by which this thread stays active without requiring the scene to resolve it.

2. Long-term care facility transfer request with residents whose care needs cannot be safely disrupted by isolation requirements

**Phase 3** includes at least one inter-institution coordination inject.

**Ground rule (bolded, mandatory in facilitator guide): This exercise is testing the system, not evaluating individuals.**

## 9. Failure Cascade & Recovery Logic — Ratified

*(Full framework from v1.1 ratified for all three projects)*

### Project 1 — Confirmed Failure Architecture

- **Single failure:** Beta's cycle initiates 0.4 seconds late — suit holds, Beta functional, Nathan does not intervene. Anomaly documented and used in debrief as evidence of real-variance performance.
- **Compound failure:** Override fails AND cycle is late — Nathan's hand moves and stops. The audience member who notices is the colleague or an intelligence observer. This is the canonical surveillance thread plant.
- **Recovery:** Demonstration succeeds on mission under compound failure. Nathan learns from variance rather than hiding it.

### Project 2 — Confirmed Failure Architecture

- **Single failure:** Intercept delay creates one real log entry — detected, retroactively flagged, attacker diverted before exploitation, no exfiltration.
- **Compound failure:** Intercept delay + civilian traffic spike + agency legal flag — Nathan invokes pre-negotiated emergency containment; all threads resolved; one behavioral anomaly remains unexplained.
- **The unrecognized anomaly is the recursive counter-simulation seed.**

### Project 3 — Confirmed Failure Architecture

- **Single failure:** Participants identify pathogen archetype early — facilitator introduces false-negative rapid test complication to re-establish uncertainty.
- **Compound failure:** Facilitator rescues + communication overwhelm + equity inject deferral — AAR must flag all three as findings, not artifacts. These are the most important findings the exercise can produce.
- **Recovery principle:** Exercise failure is exercise success if findings are honestly recorded and D4's improvement plan owner has authority to act.
