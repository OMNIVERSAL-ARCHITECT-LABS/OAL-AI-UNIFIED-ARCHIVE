## Canon Scenario: The Elimination of the Other Architects

### Core Statement (Author-Provided)

Nathan Lewis II and his brother located, engaged, and killed all other Makers — also referred to as Architects — who existed at or near the founding tier of the cosmological architecture. Following their elimination, Nathan and his brother:

1. **Absorbed** the technology and architectural frameworks belonging to each eliminated Maker/Architect completely
2. **Utilized** those absorbed systems to evolve further — advancing their own capabilities, designs, and positional authority beyond what any single Maker could have achieved independently
3. **Contained** the eliminated Architects in a dedicated environment rather than allowing permanent unstructured elimination
