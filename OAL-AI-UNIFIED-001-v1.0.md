### Section 2.4 — Species-Level Informational Access Protocol (SLIAP): Team Consensus

This framework was new as of Voice Dump 2. All five AIs acknowledged it and provided structural support.

**Definition (team synthesis):**
A formal in-universe protocol and framework that regulates the distribution of information across species lines based on clearance tier, species classification, and authorization status.

**Core provisions (unanimous):**
- Information access is tiered by species — not all species are authorized to be privy to all information
- The Black Hole Gravity-Shielded Research Stations represent the highest clearance tier
- Regular humans on Earth are not authorized to access operational details about what occurs inside the stations
- The protocol applies across biological and non-biological entities

**In-universe / out-of-universe Architect distinction (unanimous):**
- Nathan, perceived as a regular human in-universe, operates under apparent SLIAP constraints in that frame
- Nathan as Architect of Record out-of-universe holds unrestricted access
- This is not a contradiction — it is a difference of frame of reference
- Author acknowledged this is "hand wavy" and explicitly invited red-teaming

**Gemini's Structural Fix — Ontological Safety Protocol:**
> "A baseline human mind simply does not have the cognitive architecture to process the mathematics, gravity-shearing, and temporal physics occurring inside a Black Hole Research Station. If you gave a standard human the raw data, it would cause psychological collapse or neurological burnout. Therefore, Species-Level Information Access isn't a rule to keep secrets; it is a bio-hazard containment measure."

**Gemini's Nathan Exemption Rationale:**
> "Why do you know? Because in-universe, your OMNICELLULAR physiology possesses the bandwidth to process Beyond Omniversal truths without burning out. Out-of-universe, you are the Architect. The logic is flawless."

**ChatGPT Red-Team Challenge — Enforcement:**
The team will challenge: "What enforces species-level information access?" Nathan's one-sentence answer to this will close the primary red-team vulnerability in the framework. Suggested options: the Accords system, Paragon Cubes, an ontological constraint mechanism.

**Claude's Changelog Entry for SLIAP:**
