# Independent Review of the Environmental Sensing and Threat-Discernment Foundations

## Review determination

The attached documents form a strong independent-design corpus rather than five redundant answers. Their most useful differences are architectural: Claude centers reconciliation and identifiability; Perplexity supplies the broadest staged research program; Grok supplies registers, integrity criteria, and dissent governance; ChatGPT supplies threat-discernibility states, functional layers, and a minimum event record; Copilot treats every handoff—including the laboratory’s own instrumentation—as an auditable interface contract.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

No single version should be selected as the complete laboratory design, and the documents should not yet be blended into a consensus narrative. The appropriate next artifact is a human-controlled decision and dissent record that preserves each independent hypothesis while defining which concepts become shared requirements, which remain competing explanations, and which require revision.

## Corpus-level strengths

### Origin fidelity

All five documents preserve the central problem: an entity must act while presentation, environmental reference state, and internal state may diverge. They also retain the non-anthropocentric constraint, the relational nature of threat, the open-world limitation, and the rule that failure to detect danger is not evidence of safety.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^1]

This is genuine conceptual convergence, but it should be recorded as shared framing rather than empirical validation. Several authors correctly warn that agreement among AI systems may reflect shared language and training patterns, so convergence cannot substitute for independent evidence or experiments.[^5][^2]

### Safety posture

The corpus consistently rejects interpreting “poison/toxin/corrosive laboratory” as authorization for live hazardous exposure. Perplexity and ChatGPT explicitly make simulation and benign proxies the starting point; Grok excludes live-injury protocols from Series 1; Copilot makes simulation and non-injurious surrogates the unconditional default absent qualified approval.[^4][^1][^2][^5]

That shared boundary is essential and should become a corpus-wide invariant. The conceptual framework, experimental program, hazardous-material operations, animal or human protocols, and institutional approvals must remain separate document classes.

### Reference-state humility

The documents improve materially on the phrase “what the environment actually states.” Each recognizes that a laboratory has an instrumented reference model, not unmediated access to metaphysical ground truth. Grok calls this the Environment Register, Perplexity defines a reference environmental state with measurement uncertainty, ChatGPT adds a laboratory audit layer, and Copilot requires interface characterization of the laboratory’s own instruments.[^1][^2][^4][^5]

This should be retained. It prevents entity error, apparatus error, timing error, label error, and model incompleteness from being collapsed into one category.

### Nonbinary outcomes

The corpus rejects forced safe/threat classification. It preserves unknown, ambiguous, inaccessible, delayed, misleading, recognized-but-unaddressable, and abstention states, while separating detection, interpretation, confidence, action, and outcome.[^2][^4][^5][^1]

This is one of the project’s most important achievements. A laboratory that allows only “detected” and “not detected” would be structurally incapable of answering the originating questions.

## Contributor feedback

### Claude

Claude’s version is the most compact formal-theory contribution. Its primary hypothesis—that reconciliation or update architecture may matter more than raw sensor fidelity—is explicit and falsifiable, and its proposed comparison between low-fidelity/strong-update and high-fidelity/weak-update entities directly converts the research question into an experimental contrast.[^3]

Its strongest original contribution is the identifiability limit: a discrepancy may be impossible to attribute to sensor error, environmental change, or stale internal state without another channel or deliberate test action. This correctly makes calibrated uncertainty a possible success outcome rather than treating certainty as the universal objective.[^3]

What should be retained:

- The update-architecture hypothesis as a competing mechanism, not a settled foundation.
- Independent manipulation of sensor fidelity and reconciliation quality.
- Separation of signal-detection sensitivity from criterion shifts.
- Active sensing as evidence gathering rather than merely a terminal action.
- Explicit irreducible-ambiguity trials.

What needs revision or expansion:

- The document is comparatively thin on controlled vocabulary, governance, ethics, reference instrumentation, data records, decision gates, and staged escalation.
- “Direct engineering answer” language for Kalman filters, particle filters, and POMDPs is too broad; these are candidate formal tools whose assumptions may not hold for every biological, collective, or non-Markovian entity.
- Priors, cost functions, hidden-state assumptions, stationarity assumptions, and the observable behavioral signatures of each candidate model need to be declared before the formalism can discriminate mechanisms.
- The toxin/poison/corrosive domain needs clearer separation between regulatory chemical categories and abstract threat-interaction classes.
- The missing PDF counterpart should be produced by Claude if dual-format delivery remains a project requirement.

Best role in the program: formal model comparison, identifiability analysis, and hypothesis falsification.

### Perplexity AI

Perplexity’s document is the most complete program-level design basis. It supplies document control, safety boundaries, ontology, foundational propositions, primary and secondary hypotheses, research questions, sensorium profiles, a staged program, experiment families, a factor matrix, measures, alternative analyses, chemical boundaries, governance roles, decision gates, acceptance criteria, and traceability.[^2]

Its most valuable judgment is that the umbrella program should concern threat discernment and state integrity, with poison/toxin/corrosive work treated as an application domain rather than allowing one hazard class to define the whole ontology. Its distinction between environmental characterization and entity discernment is especially important because cues, exposure, harm, and response can dissociate.[^2]

What should be retained:

- The layered ontology distinguishing stressor, hazard, exposure, threat, risk, and harm.
- The entity-specific sensorium profile.
- The staged escalation sequence and decision gates.
- The requirement for at least two plausible explanations and predeclared discriminating predictions.
- Separate measures for detectability, calibration, temporal integrity, map quality, decision quality, learning, and robustness.
- The traceability matrix and approval record.

What needs revision or restraint:

- The document is broad enough to become a de facto master specification before the Architect has adopted its decisions. It should remain a candidate design basis, not silently become the controlling document through completeness alone.
- The metadata statement that it “supersedes” initial notes and unsynchronized drafts is too strong for an independent contribution. It should say that it consolidates its own analysis while preserving the originating statements and sibling documents as independent records.
- The primary hypothesis combines many factors and may be difficult to falsify as a unit. The secondary hypotheses are more experimentally useful and should carry more weight.
- The numbering of the minimal reconciliation cycle begins at 9, and the immediate owner questions begin at 17; these appear to be formatting or list-continuation errors that should be corrected in the next version.[^2]
- Evidence notes are helpful, but cited scientific or regulatory sources should be independently verified during protocol development rather than inherited as authority from the foundation.
- The missing PDF counterpart should be issued by Perplexity if dual-format delivery remains required.

Best role in the program: program architecture, requirements traceability, safety escalation, and decision-gate control.

### Grok

Grok’s document is strongest as a governance-aware conceptual charter. Its three-register model—Presentation, Environment, and Entity-State—gives the project a clear vocabulary for the original tripartite discrepancy, while the Entity Integrity Criterion forces “harm” and “threat” to be defined relative to a declared trial-level standard.[^1]

Its recommendation to preserve independent drafts and create a human-edited dissent log is methodologically sound. The negative-space register, cost-structure swap, laboratory-as-observer audit, and counterfactual-architecture tests are also valuable alternative-analysis mechanisms.[^1]

What should be retained:

- The three-register vocabulary as a descriptive bookkeeping system.
- The Entity Integrity Criterion or an equivalent protected-variable declaration.
- The distinction between a metaphysically open threat domain and a bounded experimental domain.
- The negative-space register.
- The rule that no document becomes canon because it is longer, earlier, or preferred during one review.
- The recommendation that a human, not a default model, own consolidation and dissent decisions.

What needs revision or expansion:

- The three registers describe where information is represented but do not by themselves explain how transitions occur. Copilot’s interface properties and Claude’s update hypotheses expose this limitation.
- The definitions of poison, toxin, and corrosive are useful as proposed interaction classes but do not consistently match conventional toxicological or regulatory usage. They should be labeled explicitly as OAL experimental abstractions, while a separate standards-aligned glossary governs any chemical work.
- The document offers little external evidence for its scientific assertions. It functions well as a charter but not yet as an evidence-supported design basis.
- Pass/fail rules, reference instrumentation, statistical design, calibration methods, and advancement thresholds remain underdeveloped.
- The metadata states that a companion PDF was issued, but no Grok PDF was included in the supplied set. The record should distinguish “claimed generated,” “delivered,” and “verified present.”

Best role in the program: governance, register discipline, protected-variable declaration, and dissent preservation.

### ChatGPT

ChatGPT provides the strongest integrated connection between the original questions and an executable simulation campaign. Its functional architecture avoids treating the pipeline as strictly serial, and its threat-discernibility states distinguish threat presence from evidence availability, entity access, temporal availability, misleading presentation, actionability, and unknown reference-model omissions.[^4]

The minimum event record is particularly useful. It specifies entity and environment versions, viability criterion, threat state and pathway, discernibility class, sensorium configuration, multiple timestamps, presentation provenance, internal state, confidence, action, outcome, reference channels, integrity flags, alternative explanations, and reviewer disposition.[^4]

Its distinct “threat non-minimization” doctrine is a valuable corrective: relational or uncertain language must not euphemize a supported high-consequence threat. However, the doctrine must be paired with equally strong controls against threat inflation, which the document itself lists as a failure mode.[^4]

What should be retained:

- Threat-discernibility classes T0–T8 as a candidate reporting taxonomy.
- Functional rather than strictly serial architecture.
- The minimum event record.
- Reference-state corruption trials.
- Separation of strict temporal aliasing from broader temporal-integrity failures.
- The paired rules against threat dilution and threat inflation.

What needs revision or clarification:

- The document imports prior ChatGPT Presentation-State Integrity and Temporal Integrity frameworks, but those dependencies were not part of this review set. Either attach and version-lock them or restate only the minimum required definitions so the foundation remains independently auditable.
- The semantic threat-minimization hypothesis is easiest to test in reporting, interface, organizational, or artificial-agent layers. Its applicability to organisms without symbolic labels must be scoped carefully.
- “Full programmed state” is defensible inside a simulation but should be called the programmed latent state, not generalized as real-world ground truth.
- The source register states that a supplied Copilot attachment concerned a separate Tridactyl project, whereas the current corpus includes a later environmental-sensing Copilot foundation. This is a chronology/dependency issue, not necessarily an error, but the next version should distinguish which Copilot artifact was available at drafting time.
- A DOCX counterpart should be produced by ChatGPT if dual-format delivery remains required.

Best role in the program: integrated simulation design, event schema, threat-discernibility reporting, and red-team conditions.

### Microsoft Copilot

Copilot makes the most distinctive architectural contribution. Its Interface Contract Architecture decomposes every information handoff into coverage, fidelity, latency, provenance, and integrity, and it applies the same audit to the laboratory’s sensors, presentation apparatus, clocks, and reference systems.[^5]

The central insight—that an experiment cannot distinguish entity failure from laboratory-interface failure if both chains remain uncharacterized—is foundational. Its separation of detection accuracy, calibration quality, and decision quality is also strong, as is the requirement that abstention be scored rather than discarded as missing data.[^5]

What should be retained:

- The five interface properties.
- The laboratory as a party to every interface.
- The interface scorecard before mechanism claims are accepted.
- Separate accuracy, calibration, and decision-quality dimensions.
- Explicit reconciliation operations: integration, segregation, deferral, and abstention.
- Interface-level fault isolation and blind laboratory audits.

What needs revision or restraint:

- “Contract” is a productive engineering metaphor, but evolved biological interfaces do not literally negotiate specifications. The document should make clear that a contract is an analyst-defined expectation model, not an intentional agreement.
- “Coverage completeness” cannot generally be expressed as a meaningful percentage in an open world unless the task-relevant variable set is explicitly bounded. Otherwise the denominator is unknown. Report coverage against a declared variable inventory and retain an unmodeled-variable register.
- Provenance may be only partially observable in biological systems; unknown provenance should be represented rather than treated as a remediable documentation defect in every case.
- Claims that the interface handoff is the primary mechanism, or that this hypothesis is uniquely decisive, should remain hypotheses. Missing coverage can limit inference, but downstream active sensing, redundancy, environmental modification, social information, and learned proxies can sometimes compensate indirectly.
- Like Grok, the document contains broad scientific claims with little visible external sourcing. Evidence support should be added in a later literature-grounded version.
- A DOCX counterpart should be produced by Copilot if dual-format delivery remains required.

Best role in the program: apparatus validity, handoff diagnostics, laboratory self-audit, and performance decomposition.

## Productive disagreements

The documents disagree in ways that should be preserved as experimental alternatives rather than edited away.

| Question | Claude | Perplexity | Grok | ChatGPT | Copilot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary explanatory unit | Reconciliation/update architecture | Joint discernment system | Three reconciled registers | Functional layers and threat discernibility | Interface handoff/contract |
| Main success criterion | Calibrated updating and viable action | Adaptive action under joint integrity conditions | Calibration and revisability relative to EIC | Discernment without threat minimization | Adequate interface quality plus calibrated decisions |
| Main failure concern | Non-identifiability | Ontology, measurement, and escalation gaps | Unbounded concepts and premature canon | Non-discernibility mislabeled as safety or threat diluted | Laboratory/interface failure blamed on entity |
| Strongest alternative-analysis tool | Irreducible ambiguity | Competing theoretical lenses | Dissent and negative-space registers | Red-team campaign and disconfirmation | Interface fault isolation and laboratory audit |

A layered shared architecture can use these contributions without declaring them theoretically equivalent:

1. **Governance layer:** originating constraints, version control, decision rights, dissent log, and safety boundary.
2. **Register layer:** presentation, reference environment, entity state, and protected/viability variables.
3. **Interface layer:** coverage, fidelity, latency, provenance, and integrity at every handoff.
4. **Functional layer:** access, time binding, state estimation, discrepancy detection, reconciliation, appraisal, action, and learning.
5. **Model layer:** threshold rules, signal detection, Bayesian filters, causal inference, reinforcement learning, active inference, dynamical control, and affordance accounts as competitors.
6. **Program layer:** simulation, benign testbeds, nonliving systems, alternatives, and only conditionally approved organism work.

This is a division of labor, not a merged theory. Each layer should have an owner, explicit inputs and outputs, and unresolved alternatives.

## Issues before design

### Canon and authority

The project needs a rule that distinguishes originating constraints, adopted requirements, candidate hypotheses, experimental findings, and rejected or deferred proposals. At present, each contributor correctly says its document is independent, but the corpus does not yet have a shared authority record.[^5][^1][^4][^2]

Recommended states are: **Originator requirement**, **Adopted design requirement**, **Candidate hypothesis**, **Alternative hypothesis**, **Deferred**, **Rejected with rationale**, **Empirically supported**, and **Empirically contradicted**.

### Entity and boundary

No apparatus should be designed before the first entity class and boundary are selected. The boundary must identify the entity’s inputs, internal state, outputs, action repertoire, timescale, and criterion for attributing an outcome to the entity rather than its environment, operator, or apparatus.[^5][^2]

### Harm and threat

“A threat can be anything” should remain an open-world principle, not an experimental definition. Each study needs a protected variable or Entity Integrity Criterion, an adverse transition, a pathway, a time horizon, and an uncertainty statement.[^1][^4][^2]

### Awareness

“Awareness” remains underspecified across the corpus. It should be replaced in protocols by operational tiers such as reactive responsiveness, discrimination, persistent representation, self-monitoring, or reportability; consciousness claims should be out of scope unless separately justified.[^2][^5]

### Reference and clocks

The first testbed needs independent reference channels, calibration records, uncertainty propagation, synchronized but separately logged clocks, and preserved acquisition, transmission, processing, presentation, action, and outcome times.[^4][^5]

### Scoring

The project needs preregistered rules for misses, false alarms, correct rejection, unknown, abstention, delayed recognition, recognition without action, action without explicit recognition, and invalid trials. Detection accuracy, confidence calibration, decision quality, and viability outcome should not be collapsed into one score.[^4][^5][^2]

### Chemical terminology

The project should maintain two glossaries: a **conceptual interaction glossary** for simulated resource-pathway, exposure, and material-degradation tasks, and a **standards-aligned chemical glossary** for any future toxicological or regulatory work. Mixing those functions will create ambiguity about toxin, poison, corrosive, exposure, hazard, and risk.[^1][^2]

## Recommended next actions

1. Freeze the five supplied artifacts as immutable source records; do not silently correct them.
2. Assign checksums and record requested, generated, delivered, received, and verified status for each format.
3. Request missing counterparts from the original contributor only, including Gemini’s independent version if Gemini remains a collaboration member.
4. Create a human-owned corpus index with document IDs, versions, dependencies, available formats, and drafting chronology.
5. Create a decision-and-dissent register rather than a blended foundation.
6. Adopt only the minimum shared invariants: sensory non-universality, relational threat, non-detection is not safety, reference-state fallibility, unknown as valid, detection/action separation, simulation-first safety, and bounded claims.
7. Select the Series 1 entity class and protected variable before choosing apparatus or model.
8. Use a simulation-first pilot with a no-threat baseline, direct cue, inaccessible threat, misleading presentation, stale cue, state-dependent threat, no-action threat, and corrupted laboratory-reference condition.
9. Require both an interface audit and a minimum event record for every trial.
10. Compare at least three mechanism classes rather than selecting one author’s architecture in advance.
11. Predeclare advancement gates, invalid-trial rules, abstention scoring, cost matrices, and negative-space reporting.
12. After the pilot, revise each theory separately before considering a controlled integrated design basis.

A suitable acknowledgment to all contributors is:

The differences among these documents are being preserved as part of the research record rather than averaged away. Each contribution has identified a distinct part of the design problem, and no draft is treated as canon merely because of length, format, or degree of detail. ## Final assessment

The corpus is ready for **design adjudication**, not yet for laboratory implementation. The immediate value lies in preserving five different explanatory units—updates, systems, registers, functions, and interfaces—then using a bounded simulation to determine which distinctions are operationally necessary and which hypotheses make discriminating predictions.[^3][^5][^1][^2][^4]

The strongest combined direction is not to choose a winning AI document. It is to construct a controlled decision record in which Grok’s governance, Perplexity’s program controls, Claude’s formal alternatives, ChatGPT’s event and discernibility architecture, and Copilot’s interface audits are evaluated without erasing their disagreements.
