# OAL Concept Log — Opening Epigraph Options
**Recorded:** 2026-08-04  
**Status:** All three retained for record. Option C selected for primary document use.

---

## Option A (direct and clean)

> In this system there is no response model for what follows.  
> The architecture was never required to account for it, because the possibility was treated as outside the environment by design.  
> What you are about to read is the record of what arrives when that assumption fails.

---

## Option B (slightly more ominous by implication)

> A system that declares certain knowledge impossible does not simply overlook that knowledge when it appears.  
> It has already removed the category of response that would have been needed.  
> The following pages document one such removal — and what occupied the space where the response should have been.

---

## Option C (selected for primary document — most portentous while remaining precise)

> You do not have a response model for this.  
> It is not something that can happen within the environment you were given, and therefore no contingency was ever built.  
> What follows is what occurs when the excluded possibility occurs anyway.

---

**Decision:** Option C used as the opening epigraph in the formal Concept Log v2.1 (and subsequent standing reference editions). Options A and B retained here for future alternate editions or side-by-side comparison.
