## 1. Executive Summary

Volumes I through III established method, historical iterations, and five alternate timelines. Volume IV maps the relationships that make the source-recorded archive and its comparative histories cohere, conflict, or remain deliberately unresolved.

**Central finding:** The archive behaves as a layered system: source authority controls what can be stated; cosmological premises constrain capability; capability enables institutions and scenes; scenes generate questions; and provenance/versioning controls how questions re-enter the archive.

### High-gravity conclusions

- **Authorial ratification and provenance** are the governance root.
- **Cosmological duality and apex boundaries** shape high-tier relationships and moral framing.
- **Post-scarcity and the Abundance Set** link currencies, research, infrastructure, and capability.
- **OMNICELLULAR / no-switching / 4,712** link survival architecture to evidence and rescue operations.
- **Founding succession / absorption / containment** is high sensitivity but remains [A][B] pending ratification.
- **The First Memory** is a narrative bridge node with several author-only dependencies.
- **Translation, dissent, and withheld material** are structural safeguards against false certainty.

## 3. Dependency Method

Decision gravity is assessed qualitatively through reach, dependency strength, consequence of change, and provenance sensitivity. High gravity means expensive to change without broad ripple effects; it does not mean artistically superior.

## 10. Change-Control and Impact Protocol

Before changing a high-gravity node: classify the source, locate the nodes/routes touched, test against hard rules and boundaries, scope the change, preserve uncertainty/dissent, and create a versioned reconciliation register.
