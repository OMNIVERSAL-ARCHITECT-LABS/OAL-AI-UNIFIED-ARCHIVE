SLIAP: Species Level Information Access Protocol
This folder contains preliminary research, foundational proposals, and adversarial analyses supporting the development of SLIAP, the Species Level Information Access Protocol. The project explores how information access, exchange, protection, and governance might operate among species, civilizations, and other forms of intelligence without assuming human superiority or humanity’s right to establish universal rules.

Contributions examine consent, representation, autonomy, privacy, unequal capabilities, and the risks of coercion, exclusion, and concentrated authority. The research also questions whether species-level governance—or a unified protocol—is an appropriate foundation at all.

Materials include independently generated AI research reports, comparative evaluations, working drafts, and supporting references in Markdown, PDF, and DOCX formats. These documents are exploratory contributions intended for critical review and revision, not an adopted standard or a claim to speak on behalf of any species or civilization.
